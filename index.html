About

From statistical physics to quantitative finance.

My research background is in statistical physics and complex systems, where I study how microscopic stochastic fluctuations can produce collective behaviour, extreme events and systemic failure.

My doctoral research investigated failure, vulnerability, resilience and intervention in complex networks using analytical modelling, stochastic simulation and large-scale computational experiments.

I am now extending these ideas toward quantitative finance, with interests in financial markets, market microstructure, stochastic modelling, portfolio risk and systemic risk.
